Business & Contract Dispute Mediation

Resolve disputes between business partners, vendors, and clients without the cost, delay, and strain of a lawsuit.

Business disputes, whether between partners, vendors, or clients, can quickly become expensive and time-consuming if they end up in litigation. Mediation offers a faster, more private alternative: a neutral mediator helps both sides work through the disagreement and reach terms they can both live with, often without derailing the business relationship entirely.

Common Business Disputes Suited to Mediation

  • Partnership and shareholder disagreements
  • Breach of contract claims
  • Vendor and supplier disputes
  • Client billing or scope disagreements
  • Disputes over the terms of a sale or dissolution of a business

Why Mediation Makes Sense for Business Disputes

  • Cost-effective. Litigation between businesses can run into significant legal fees; mediation typically resolves matters in a fraction of the time and cost.
  • Confidential. Business disputes often involve sensitive financial or competitive information that neither side wants in a public court record.
  • Preserves relationships. Especially important between ongoing business partners, vendors, or clients who may need to keep working together.
  • Faster. Business disputes are often time-sensitive, and mediation can resolve them in days or weeks rather than the months or years litigation can take.

With more than a decade of experience in contract negotiation and commercial agreements, mediation for business and contract disputes benefits from a mediator who understands the underlying deal terms, not just the mediation process itself.

How It Works

The process is simple: contact us, schedule a session, and mediate. Before your session, both parties review and sign an Agreement to Mediate that outlines confidentiality and the ground rules for the process. There's no separate consultation step to schedule first, you can go straight from reaching out to getting a session on the calendar.
