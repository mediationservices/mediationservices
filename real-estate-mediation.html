Real Estate Mediation

Resolve disputes between buyers, sellers, lenders, and agents without the delay and expense of litigation.

Real estate transactions involve a lot of moving parts, buyers, sellers, lenders, and agents, and disputes can arise at any stage. When they do, mediation offers a faster and more collaborative way to resolve the issue than heading straight to litigation.

Benefits of Real Estate Mediation

  • Lower cost. Mediation typically involves only the parties and a neutral mediator, avoiding the expense of prolonged litigation.
  • Faster. Real estate disputes are often time-sensitive, and mediation can be completed in a matter of hours or days rather than months.
  • Collaborative. Instead of an adversarial court process, both sides work together toward a solution.
  • Confidential. Sensitive financial and personal details stay out of the public record.
  • Preserves relationships. Useful when the parties, such as neighboring property owners or ongoing business partners, need to maintain a working relationship after the dispute is resolved.

With a background that includes years of real estate and contract negotiation experience, mediation for real estate disputes benefits from a mediator who understands the practical details of these transactions, not just the mediation process itself.

How It Works

The process is simple: contact us, schedule a session, and mediate. Before your session, both parties review and sign an Agreement to Mediate that outlines confidentiality and the ground rules for the process. There's no separate consultation step to schedule first, you can go straight from reaching out to getting a session on the calendar.
