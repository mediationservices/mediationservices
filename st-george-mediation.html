St. George Mediation Services

Confidential, professional mediation serving St. George and Southern Utah, with remote sessions available for anyone who can't meet in person.

If you're facing a dispute in St. George or elsewhere in Southern Utah, mediation is often a faster and less stressful path than going to court. A neutral mediator helps both sides communicate and find a resolution that works, without the cost and formality of litigation.

What Can Be Mediated

  • Divorce and family law disputes
  • Business and contract disputes
  • Real estate disputes
  • Personal injury and civil claims
  • Neighbor and community disputes

How the Process Works

The process is straightforward: contact us to describe your situation, schedule a session, and review and sign an Agreement to Mediate before you meet. Sessions can be held in person in St. George or remotely, whichever works best for both parties.

Why Choose Mediation in St. George

Mediation is typically faster, less expensive, and less stressful than going to court, and it puts the outcome in your hands rather than a judge's. As a Utah court-rostered mediator and licensed attorney, sessions are grounded in real legal experience, not just facilitation training.
