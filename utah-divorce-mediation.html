Utah Divorce Mediation

Work through custody, parenting time, and property division with your spouse in a structured, respectful setting, without the cost and stress of a courtroom.

If you're going through a divorce in Utah, mediation offers a way to reach a settlement with your spouse without the expense, delay, and adversarial tone of a courtroom battle. A mediator doesn't take sides or make decisions for you. Instead, the mediator helps you and your spouse communicate clearly, work through the issues, and reach an agreement that reflects both of your priorities.

In Utah, divorce mediation is often required before a case can proceed to trial, precisely because it tends to produce faster, less costly, and less contentious outcomes than litigation.

What Divorce Mediation Covers

  • Division of assets and debts
  • Child custody and parenting time
  • Child support and spousal support
  • Any other terms that need to be resolved before finalizing the divorce

Why Choose Mediation Over Litigation

Mediation is typically less expensive than litigation, since it requires fewer court appearances and less preparation. It's also less adversarial, which matters when you and your spouse will continue to co-parent or otherwise interact after the divorce. And because both spouses have a say in the outcome, agreements reached in mediation tend to hold up better over time than terms imposed by a court.

Working with a mediator who is also a licensed Utah attorney means the process is grounded in a practical understanding of family law and what a workable agreement actually needs to include.

How It Works

The process is simple: contact us, schedule a session, and mediate. Before your session, both parties review and sign an Agreement to Mediate that outlines confidentiality and the ground rules for the process. There's no separate consultation step to schedule first, you can go straight from reaching out to getting a session on the calendar.
